# Privacy Policy

**Estate Manager Pro**  
Last updated: May 27, 2026

---

## Overview

Estate Manager Pro ("the App") is a mobile application for managing estate tasks, equipment inventory, and team members. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

---

## Data We Collect

### Account Information
- **Email address** — used to create and identify your account
- **Display name** — shown to other estate members
- **Password** — stored securely via Supabase Auth (hashed, never visible to us)

### Profile Information
- **Timezone** — used to display dates and times correctly for your location
- **Push notification token** — used to send you task assignment notifications

### User-Generated Content
- **Tasks** — titles, descriptions, due dates, status, photos attached to tasks
- **Inventory items** — names, vendor info, location, descriptions, photos
- **Categories** — names and colors you create to organize tasks

### Technical Data
- Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) and is associated with your account and your estate

---

## How We Use Your Data

- To provide the core app functionality: managing tasks, inventory, and team members
- To send push notifications when tasks are assigned to you (only if you grant permission)
- To identify you within your estate and display your name and avatar to other members

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** use your data for advertising.

---

## Data Sharing

Your data is shared only with:

- **Other members of your estate** — they can see tasks, inventory, and member names/emails within the shared estate
- **Supabase** — our database and authentication provider. [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Expo** — our push notification service. [Expo Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)

No other third parties receive your data.

---

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored in Supabase's secure PostgreSQL infrastructure
- Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth with industry-standard hashing
- Row-level security (RLS) ensures you can only access data belonging to your estate
- We do not store payment information

---

## Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see below), all your data is permanently removed.

---

## Deleting Your Account and Data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly within the app:

1. Open the App
2. Go to **Profile** (bottom navigation)
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete My Account**
4. Confirm the deletion

This action is **irreversible** and will immediately remove your account, profile, and all data you created. If you are the sole member of an estate, the estate and all its data will also be deleted.

---

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data (via the Profile screen)
- Delete your data (via the in-app account deletion)
- Export your data (contact us)

---

This App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
